Privacy Policy (High Low Ball)

Last updated: 2026-08-22

This Privacy Policy explains how HyperCasting ("we", "the developer") collects, uses, and stores information through the mobile game "High Low Ball" ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number. Instead, it uses anonymous authentication via Supabase (our backend service), which issues a random anonymous user ID per device. Under that ID, we store:

DataPurpose
Anonymous user ID (not personally identifiable)Internal identifier to distinguish records/rankings across devices
Stage clear records, best timesSave progress, compute rankings
Play replay data (ball trajectory, drawn line coordinates)Replay sharing / spectating features
Heart (retry count) usage historySync in-game currency state

The App does not request sensitive device permissions such as location, contacts, camera, or microphone, and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Storage and Processing

Collected information is stored via our backend provider, Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure). See Supabase's privacy policy at https://supabase.com/privacy for details.

3. Information Sharing

Replay data may be visible to other players for in-game ranking/spectating features. However, replays belonging to the top/bottom 10% of results are automatically hidden by the server and never exposed to other users. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties beyond what is described here.

4. Data Retention

Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete data stored on our servers. To request deletion of your data, please contact us with your anonymous user ID as described below.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 14. All data collected by the App is limited to non-identifying gameplay data as described above.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to or deletion of your game data at any time. Because anonymous authentication does not carry a recoverable identity, please include any identifying context you can provide (e.g., a screenshot) to help us process deletion requests.
